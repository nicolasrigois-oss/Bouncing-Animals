How to play

Paused. Close this to carry on.

  • Pick who startsOn the start screen, tap ‹ or › beside the dancer to choose the first animal.
  • PlayYour animal floats in a soap bubble: slide your finger to move the dished platform and bounce it into the glass bricks to free its friends. Blue bricks hold a friend who joins in, in its own bubble: bounce it out through a door, and don't let it fall in the water either, or you lose a life. Pink bricks hold Bad Hip and his water bombs: dodge them, or they fill your boat! Free every trapped friend to win the level. 10 levels.
    Bad HipA masked little Hip sneaks in with a bucket, a hose or water balloons… watch out! On the dance stage, tap the mask button for his hose or water balloons.
    Dance stageWatch your animal's five moves. Tap one at the top to dance along; tap the guest to send it back.
  • SwipeSend the dancer off at the start. Later, swipe any animal to steer it.
  • Flick to spinSideways spins it round. Up or down flips it. Faster flicks spin faster.
  • Twist two fingersRolls an animal like a wheel.
  • TapA surprise spin for everyone.
  • Speed and Bumpers slidersChange the speed, and add up to 5 pop bumpers for the animals to bounce off.
  • Drag a bumperTouch a bumper and slide your finger to move it.
